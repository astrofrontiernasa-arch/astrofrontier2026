Part 1 · Mars

Spirit & Opportunity

SPIRIT

Mars Landing Year
2004
Lifetime
6 years, 2 months, 19 days
Distance Traveled
7.73 km (4.8 miles)
Images Captured
More than 124,000
Fun Facts
  • Drove backward for years after its front wheel broke.
  • Extended its 90-day mission to over six years.
  • First robot to grind a rock and climb a hill on another planet.
  • Accidentally uncovered proof of ancient hot springs.

OPPORTUNITY

Mars Landing Year
2004
Lifetime
14 years, 4 months (5,111 sols)
Distance Travelled
45.16 km (28.06 miles)
Images Captured
More than 217,000
Fun Facts
  • Kid-Named: Named by a 9-year-old girl, who named both twins.
  • Hole in One: Bounced 26 times, then rolled into a tiny crater.
  • Marathon Runner: First to drive a full marathon on another world.
  • Meteorite Finder: Found the first meteorite ever seen on another planet.

Part 2 · The Moon

Eagle

EAGLE

Moon Landing Year
1969 (Apollo 11 Lunar Module Eagle)
Lifetime on Surface
21 hours, 36 minutes
Distance Traveled
0 km: Eagle is a lander, so it stayed in one place. The astronauts explored on foot, going as far as Little West Crater, about 60 m away.
Images Captured
About 1,400 photos during the mission, taken by the astronauts with Hasselblad cameras
Fun Facts
  • Landed with less than 45 seconds of fuel left.
  • The golden bottom half (the “descent stage”) still sits on the Moon today.
  • The astronauts accidentally broke the engine arming switch…
  • …so Buzz Aldrin used a felt-tip pen to push it in, and they launched back into space!
